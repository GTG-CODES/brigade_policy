Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Information Collection

We collect personal data you provide directly to us when you create an account, update your profile, or participate in the community forums. This collected information includes your full name, email address, phone number (optional), military/organizational rank, company, and battalion details. Additionally, when you interact with our features, we collect the content of your posts, comments, media uploads, and messages.

2. Use of Information

The information collected is used solely to provide, operate, maintain, and improve the services of The Boys' Brigade App. This includes authenticating user accounts, facilitating secure peer communication, processing forum discussions, analyzing usage patterns to optimize performance, and delivering important system updates and support notifications.

3. Third-Party AI Services

Our app includes an AI assistant ("Anchor AI") powered by third-party technologies. When you utilize the AI chat feature, the text messages you input are transmitted to Google (Gemini) and OpenAI (ChatGPT) to generate intelligent responses. To protect your privacy, no personal profile identifiers (such as your name, email, phone number, or company) are shared with these services. Only the textual content of the chat query is transmitted.

4. Third-Party Data Sharing & Analytics

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with external third parties for marketing purposes. Data sharing is strictly restricted to operations necessary to run the app:

  • Administration: Administrative data may be shared with The Boys' Brigade organization for membership validation purposes.
  • Advertising: We integrate Google AdMob to deliver ads. AdMob collects basic device identifiers and location metadata to serve relevant advertisements.
  • Analytics & Performance: We use Firebase and Sentry to monitor crash diagnostics and analyze general application trends to enhance platform stability.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security safeguards to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, theft, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, please be aware that no transmission method over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal data as long as your account remains active. You possess complete control over your information and can initiate account deletion at any time via the settings panel in the app. Upon confirming deletion, your Auth account is deleted, your Firestore user profile is cleared, and all your personal identifiers are removed. To preserve community discussion integrity, any forum posts, comments, or chat logs you published will remain on the platform but will be permanently anonymized and marked as authored by "Anonymous User".

7. Cookies and Tracking

We may utilize local cookies, shared preferences, and tracking tokens to manage session persistence, store application configuration preferences, and monitor view token balances for video streams.

8. Changes to Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will become effective immediately upon posting. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date above, posting updates directly within the app, or publishing the revised policy at our hosted URL: https://gtg-codes.github.io/brigade_policy/

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or our data handling practices, please contact us directly at gtgcodes@gmail.com.
